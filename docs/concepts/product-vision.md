## 🚗 Vision

Israride is a ride-hailing platform designed for a future legal framework in Israel that allows private drivers to offer transportation services.

The platform is built to support a regulated market where private ride services become legal, transparent, and compliant with government requirements.

It is positioned as an alternative to traditional platforms like Uber, focusing on a more transparent pricing model and a fairer economic structure for drivers.

---

## 🌍 Market Context

Private ride-hailing services (similar to Uber) are currently restricted in Israel but are expected to become legal under a new regulatory initiative.

This creates a new market opportunity for platforms that are:

- compliant with local regulation  
- adapted to multilingual users  
- designed with transparency and fairness  

---

## ⚖️ Key Product Idea

Unlike traditional ride-hailing platforms, Israride is based on:

- no commission per ride  
- subscription-based platform access for drivers  
- transparent pricing (distance + time + regulatory fees)  
- adaptability to evolving legislation  

---

## 👤 Target Users

### Passengers
- Residents of Israel  
- Tourists  
- Users requiring multilingual interface  
- Price-sensitive riders  

### Drivers
- Licensed taxi drivers  
- Independent private drivers  
- Drivers seeking reduced platform commissions  

---

## 💡 Value Proposition

### For Passengers
- Transparent pricing  
- Clear ride cost estimation  
- Multi-language interface (RU, EN, HE, AR)  

### For Drivers
- Fair and predictable revenue model  
- No percentage-based commission  
- Greater independence from platform control  

---

## 🚀 Product Direction

The platform is designed to evolve into a full ride-hailing ecosystem, including:

- passenger and driver mobile applications  
- real-time ride matching  
- scalable backend infrastructure  
- regulatory-compliant pricing system
